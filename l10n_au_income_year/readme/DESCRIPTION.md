Helpers for the standard Australian income year, 1 July to 30 June.

`l10n_au` sets an Australian company's fiscal year to end 30 June. This
module labels the income year that a given date falls in, and flags
whether a company is on the standard year at all, so other AU addons
have one place to ask rather than recomputing the dates.

This is the standard income year only. It is not the FBT year (1 April
to 31 March) and it is not a substituted accounting period. PASS on the
flag is not lodgment or close approval.
